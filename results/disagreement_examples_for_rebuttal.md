# Disagreement examples for rebuttal / appendix

The 9 disagreements in the independent annotation check are all one-directional:
original `slogan` -> independent `substantive`.  This suggests boundary cases
rather than a collapse of the annotation scheme.  Two representative examples:

## Example 1: aggregate facts embedded in political framing

Original label: `slogan`  
Independent label: `substantive`

Short excerpt:

> In 2018, enterprises under the state-owned-assets supervision system generated 12.4 trillion yuan in added value, nearly one-seventh of national GDP; central enterprises generated 7.1 trillion yuan, nearly one-twelfth of national GDP. During 2013--2018, the system paid 19.6 trillion yuan in taxes, 24.9% of national tax revenue.

Why it is a boundary case:

The paragraph is embedded in a political discussion of the historical role of SOEs, so the original annotator treated it as part of the slogan/register block.  The independent annotator focused on the concrete statistics and classified it as substantive.  This is a reasonable disagreement: the passage is not empty rhetoric, but it also does not express a firm-specific leader stance.

## Example 2: concrete reform measures following political setup

Original label: `slogan`  
Independent label: `substantive`

Short excerpt:

> SASAC promoted central enterprises to streamline operations and improve efficiency. By the first half of 2019, 2,041 subsidiaries included in the zombie-firm and distressed-firm programme had returned to overall profitability, generating more than 200 billion yuan in loss reduction and profit increase compared with 2015. Central enterprises had reduced steel capacity by 16.14 million tons and coal capacity by 106 million tons.

Why it is a boundary case:

The paragraph begins from a policy-rhetorical frame, but it contains concrete measures, time points, numbers, and outcomes.  The independent annotator therefore labelled it substantive.  This disagreement supports our interpretation that the original labels were conservative: when in doubt, the original annotation tended not to count politically framed content as substantive, even when it contained concrete operational evidence.

## Rebuttal sentence

The 9 disagreements are interpretable boundary cases rather than evidence of annotation failure: they are passages where concrete actions, statistics, or reform outcomes are embedded in political rhetoric.  This direction of disagreement is conservative for our evaluation, because no original `substantive` segment was relabelled as `slogan` or `irrelevant`.
